Kashmiri pashmina begins with fine mountain fibre and passes through skilled hands.

Read about Kashmir

From the high plateau to Srinagar

Changthangi goats grow a fine winter undercoat on the high plateau. In Kashmir, specialists sort the fibre, spin it on the yinder, dye the yarn, weave the cloth, and add hand embroidery.

From fibre to finished shawl

This light orientation follows four broad movements. The full Craft page keeps the eight stages visible while making clear that workshop practice varies.

  1. 01

    Fibre

    Pashm is gathered, cleaned, and sorted for spinning.

  2. 02

    Yarn

    Fine fibre is spun and prepared in the intended colours.

  3. 03

    Cloth

    Warp and weft meet on the loom to form the textile.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Surface work, washing, finishing, and inspection complete the route.
